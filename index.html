    Fan-made and unofficial; not affiliated with BIGHIT MUSIC, HYBE, iMe Indonesia, Live Nation or GBK. Categories, prices and where each category sits follow iMe Indonesia's seat map (prices exclude tax and admin fees; every category has numbered seats, and all three shows are sold out). The stage follows reviews of the tour: a 360° stage with a pavilion modelled on Gyeonghoeru at the centre and runways that recall the four trigrams of the Korean flag. Exact block edges, sector numbers, row counts, stage and screen sizes, and where the roof ends are estimates, so use the official map to confirm your seat. Performers are generic stand-ins, not likenesses.
